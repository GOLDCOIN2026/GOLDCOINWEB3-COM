Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

GoldCoin is committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring a safe, secure, and fair experience for every user. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the GoldCoin app and related services.

📁 Information We Collect

1. Account Information

  • Email address for login and communication
  • User ID generated by our system
  • Referral activity and referral rewards
  • Your daily mining and boost activity

2. Device Information

  • Device model and OS version
  • Unique device identifiers (for anti-fraud)
  • Network type and country
  • App performance and crash logs

3. Usage Data

  • Mining start/stop timestamps (server-time only)
  • Rewarded ad views (for Boost & Spin)
  • Coins collected, shifted to wallet, or withdrawn
  • Game activity (Spin, Miner, Catch Coin, Bounce)

4. Security & Anti-Cheat Data

  • Modified app/APK detection logs
  • Multiple account detection
  • Suspicious activity flags (speed hack, fake time, auto click)
  • IP abuse prevention

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the GoldCoin mining and reward system
  • To track your daily 2 free coin reward
  • To manage wallet balance, withdrawals & referral bonuses
  • To prevent cheating, hacks, multi-device abuse & fraud
  • To operate ads for rewarded boosts and spin rewards
  • To store mining data using secure Firestore servers
  • To send important updates (maintenance, version alerts)
  • To improve app performance and add new features

🛡️ Data Protection & Security

Encryption

All sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protection.

Server-Timestamp System

GoldCoin never uses device time. We rely on secure server timestamps to prevent manipulation.

Anti-Cheat & Fraud Protection

Fake coins, time changes, speed hacks, APK modifications, auto-click tools, and multi-account abuse are automatically detected and blocked.

Regular Security Updates

We update the app frequently to improve protection and maintain a fair reward system.
